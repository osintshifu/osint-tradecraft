## How to Use

1. Open the relevant prompt and copy its contents into your research environment.
2. Complete the target and any required scope or authorization fields.
3. Review the findings against the cited sources and the tools actually used.

Available tools and source access determine what can be checked. The prompt does not provide access to external services.
